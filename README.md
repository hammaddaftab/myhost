# MyHost — Project Requirements & Architecture

Requirements specification and conceptual breakdown for the **MyHost** short-term rental (STR) management and co-hosting platform.

---

## 1. Process / How It Works

> **Original Phrasing:**
> ```text
> How It Works (Process)
> Simple 3-4 step visual:
> - Free consultation & property audit
> - Custom plan (comms, listing, review strategy)
> - Ongoing management/support
> - Regular performance reports
> ```

### Structured Breakdown
1. **Audit & Consultation**: Complimentary initial consultation and comprehensive STR property audit.
2. **Custom Strategy Plan**: Tailored roadmap addressing guest communications, listing optimization, and review management.
3. **Active Management**: Daily operations, round-the-clock guest messaging, and support coordination.
4. **Performance Reporting**: Scheduled reporting cycles delivering insights on occupancy, revenue, ratings, and resolution stats.

---

## 2. Differentiators / Why Choose MyHost

> **Original Phrasing:**
> ```text
> Why Choose MyHost (Differentiators)
> - Faster guest response times = better ratings
> - Proven review dispute/removal process
> - Data-backed listing optimization
> - Dedicated support, not outsourced call centers
> ```

### Structured Breakdown
- **Rapid Guest Response Times**: Substantially faster response SLAs to improve response rate metrics and guest review scores.
- **Review Dispute & Removal**: A structured, proven process to dispute and remove unfair or policy-violating negative reviews.
- **Data-Backed Listing Optimization**: Algorithmic and market-data adjustments to photos, titles, descriptions, and dynamic pricing.
- **Dedicated In-House Support**: Assigned account management and guest support rather than impersonal outsourced call centers.

## 3. Pricing / Packages

> **Original Phrasing:**
> ```text
> Pricing/Packages
> - Tiered plans (Basic comms only / Full-service / Custom for portfolios)
> - Highlight the free-first-5 offer again here with clear terms
> ```

### Structured Breakdown
- **Tiered Service Packages**:
  - **Basic**: Guest communications and message handling only.
  - **Full-Service**: Comprehensive listing management, guest messaging, optimization, and dispute resolution.
  - **Custom Portfolio**: Bespoke agreements designed for multi-unit operators and property management groups.
- **Introductory Incentive**:
  - Highlighted **"Free-First-5"** offer clearly detailing qualifying terms and scope.

## 5. FAQ Section

> **Original Phrasing:**
> ```text
> 8. FAQ Section
> - Do you work with multiple platforms?
> - How does review removal work / what's your success rate?
> - Do I keep control of my listing?
> - What happens after the free consultation?
> ```

### Structured Breakdown
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Cross-platform synchronization and management coverage across major OTA channels (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, etc.).
- **Review Removal Mechanics**: Clarity on platform policies, legitimate dispute processes, and historical success rates.
- **Listing Ownership & Control**: Guarantees that hosts retain full ownership and administrative control over their accounts and assets.
- **Post-Consultation Process**: Transparent expectations of what occurs immediately following the free consultation.
